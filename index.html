About the Ground

Sillypoint is a dedicated cricket ground designed for practice sessions, matches, tournaments, and community sports events.

Named after the fielding position that demands sharp reflexes and courage, Sillypoint Ground brings the same spirit to every player who steps onto the pitch.

Whether you're booking a net session, organizing a weekend match, or hosting a full-scale sports event, the ground is maintained to a high standard year-round.

Open to individuals, clubs, schools, and corporate teams looking for a reliable, well-kept venue.
